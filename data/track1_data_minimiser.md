# Track 1 – Data Minimiser
**AI Assistant for Privacy-by-Design Forms & Flows (hard-coded)**

*Legal Hackathon – Responsible AI for Sensitive Data · 23 September 2026*

---

## Why
Data minimisation is a core principle of responsible data processing, but hard to apply in day-to-day product design. Goal: a tool that makes it easier to **collect only what is needed**, **communicate that clearly**, and **reduce risk from the start**.

## The Problem
- Products, onboarding flows and internal systems collect more data than necessary.
- Data is stored too long, and sensitive data is collected without a clear purpose.
- **Task:** Build an AI assistant that reviews forms, onboarding journeys or CRM fields and identifies where data can be minimised, where sensitive data is over-collected, and how to explain data use more clearly.

## What the Solution Should Do
Support product, legal/compliance and operations teams by:
- Flagging unnecessary or overly intrusive fields
- Identifying sensitive or special-category data
- Suggesting simpler alternatives or optional fields
- Generating plain-language microcopy explaining why each field is needed
- Mapping data flows through CRM, analytics, support or third-party tools

## What the Jury Is Looking For
- A prototype that reviews a sample form, flow or data schema
- Clear identification of fields to remove, make optional, or explain better
- Suggestions for improved wording or data-handling logic
- Strong UX that makes privacy decisions easy to understand
- Responsible AI design: human review, transparency, data minimisation built in

> The best solutions reduce friction for users **and** help organisations apply privacy-by-design in a practical, everyday way.

## Success Signal (Evaluation)
The tool classifies each field as:

| Keep | Make optional | Remove | Better explain |
|------|---------------|--------|----------------|

…and gives **understandable reasons** — **without penalising inclusive optional design choices** (e.g. optional pronoun or accessibility fields).
